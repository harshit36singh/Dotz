# Privacy Policy for dotz

**Effective date:** 11 November 2026

This policy explains how the dotz Android app ("dotz", "the app", package `com.wallpaper.dotz`) handles your information. In short: **dotz does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data.** Everything you enter stays on your device.

## Information you enter

To draw your wallpaper, you can enter:

- the view you want (Year, Month, Goal or Life) and how it looks (colours, shape, grid, label)
- a goal name and its start and target dates
- your date of birth and a life expectancy (Life view)
- names and dates of yearly marked dates, such as birthdays
- your own label text
- a background photo you pick from your gallery

All of this is saved **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. If you pick a background photo, the app keeps a copy of just that photo in its private storage so the wallpaper can draw it. The app uses the system photo picker, so it never gets access to the rest of your photos.

None of this information is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

## Network use: daily quote

If you set the label to **Quote**, the app and the live wallpaper request the quote of the day from the [ZenQuotes API](https://zenquotes.io) about once a day (and when you tap "New quote"). The request contains no personal information from the app. Like any internet request, it reveals your device's IP address to ZenQuotes, which handles it under its own terms and privacy practices. If you don't use Quote mode, the app makes no network requests at all.

## What dotz does not do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No advertising and no advertising IDs
- No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions
- No selling or sharing of data with third parties

## Permissions

| Permission | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Internet | Fetching the daily quote, only in Quote mode |
| Run at startup (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`) | Redrawing the home-screen widget after the phone restarts |

## Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's settings in your device backup, which Google stores in your Google account under Google's own privacy policy. The developer has no access to these backups.

## Google Play

When you download dotz from Google Play, Google may collect information (such as install statistics) under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The developer only sees aggregated, anonymous statistics that Google Play Console provides.

## Deleting your data

Because your data never leaves your device, you control it fully. To delete it, clear the app's storage (Settings → Apps → dotz → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the app.

## Children

dotz is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
